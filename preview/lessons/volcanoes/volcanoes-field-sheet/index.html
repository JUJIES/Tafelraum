Why live so close?

Use observations and evidence to explain a difficult decision.

1 · Watch: what does the evidence show?

Ridgehaven: a fictional village

Ridgehaven stands near an active volcano. Old volcanic material has weathered into fertile soil. The Ali family grows vegetables and earns extra money guiding visitors. Their relatives live nearby. A home elsewhere would cost more than they can afford.

Past lava flows have damaged roads. Volcanic gases can harm health. Scientists monitor the volcano, but cannot promise that the village will always be safe. The family has an evacuation plan.

This invented case is not a description of Kīlauea or the people in the film. Fertile soil develops over time; fresh lava is not farmland.

2 · Investigate: read the fictional case

Work with a partner. Record short phrases from the case.

3 · Explain: why might the family stay?

Write three sentences: a benefit, a risk, and a limit on their choices. Use because and however.
