# Historical teaching surface study

The Calm Dusk study held the layout, material and functions from round two constant while comparing dark graphite and muted night blue with the lighter surface. Off-white text and restrained blue/yellow accents needed hardware contrast checks. Original images and PDFs must retain their colours when themes change.

These mockups were discussion material, not a pixel specification. The original screenshots contained lesson artwork without documented redistribution rights and are not included. See the [teaching surfaces brief](../../02_TEACHING_SURFACES_UI_BRIEF.md).
